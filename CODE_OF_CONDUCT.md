# Community Code of Conduct

CampaignKit welcomes people of different backgrounds, skill levels and languages. Help others participate and criticize ideas or code respectfully.

## Expected behavior

- Be patient with first-time contributors and ask before assuming intent.
- Give specific, constructive feedback and credit other people's work.
- Respect privacy. Use invented examples rather than publishing someone's campaign data.
- Keep discussions relevant and allow maintainers to close repeated or off-topic threads.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexualized unwanted attention, doxxing and repeated personal attacks are not acceptable. Neither are spam and misrepresenting other people's work as your own.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer's [GitHub profile](https://github.com/diyaryavuz) using an available contact method. If no private contact is available, do not post sensitive details publicly; request a private contact through a minimal issue without names or incident details. This project does not currently provide a dedicated confidential reporting mailbox or an independent appeal body.

Diyar Yavuz is currently responsible for moderation. Depending on severity, actions may include a request to edit content, a warning, removing content, closing a discussion, or restricting participation. The maintainer should explain an action where doing so does not disclose private information. Participants may ask for reconsideration with additional context.

This policy applies in the repository's issues, PRs and other project-managed spaces. It is a project policy, not a claim of affiliation with an external code-of-conduct organization.
